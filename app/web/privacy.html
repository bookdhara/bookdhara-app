BookDhara Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · This is an early version of BookDhara and this policy may change. We will update the date above when it does.

BookDhara (bookdhara.github.io/bookdhara-app) is an app for buying and selling used books in India. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name. Your phone number is optional.
  • Login details: if you set a password, we store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, never the password itself. If you use "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We do not get your Google password or access to your Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Listings: books you list, including the photos you upload, price, condition and city.
  • Orders and credits: what you buy and sell, delivery choice, the delivery address you enter for courier orders, and your BookDhara credit history.
  • Technical data: basic server logs, such as your IP address and the time of a request, used to keep the service running and to stop abuse like repeated wrong logins.

How we use it

  • To create your account and log you in, including emailing you one-time login codes.
  • To show your listings to other users and complete orders between buyers and sellers.
  • To share what is needed for an order: a buyer and seller see each other's name, and the seller sees the delivery address for courier orders.
  • To keep BookDhara safe, for example by limiting login attempts.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Services we use

We use a few outside services to run BookDhara. They handle data only to provide their service to us:

  • Render – runs our server.
  • Neon – stores our database (servers in Singapore).
  • Brevo – sends login code emails.
  • Google – "Continue with Google" sign-in, and book details looked up by ISBN (only the ISBN is sent).
  • GitHub Pages – hosts this website and the app.

Payments

BookDhara is currently in test mode and does not collect real payments. When real payments are added, they will be handled by a payment provider, and we will update this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. Order and credit records may be kept longer where needed for disputes or the law.

Your choices

  • You can edit your name and phone number in the app (Profile).
  • You can remove your listings at any time.
  • To get a copy of your data or delete your account, email us at the address below. We will reply within 30 days.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords and limits on login attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Children

BookDhara is meant for people aged 18 and over, or younger users with a parent or guardian's permission.
